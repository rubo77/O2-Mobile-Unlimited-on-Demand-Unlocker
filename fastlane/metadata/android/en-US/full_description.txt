O2 Free mit Weiter Speed Unlocker sends an SMS with a freely definable text to a freely definable number at a configurable interval (minutes or hours, minimum 10 minutes) — completely automatically, even in the background.

<b>Typical use case: O2 Unlimited On Demand</b>
The O2 "Mobile Unlimited On Demand" tariff includes 10 GB of high-speed data per day. Once 80 % is used, replying "WEITER" to 80112 books another free 2 GB block — repeatable without limit. This app automates exactly that: send "WEITER" to 80112 every 30 minutes without thinking about it.

<b>Features</b>
- Configurable recipient number, message text and interval (minimum 10 minutes as abuse guard)
- Optional random delay (default 10%, up to 100% of the interval); SMS are never sent earlier than the selected interval
- Optional end date/time and SMS-count limit; the service stops when either configured condition is reached
- Foreground service keeps sending while the app is in the background
- Long texts are automatically split into multipart SMS
- In-app send log and live status (sent count, countdown to next send)
- Inputs are persisted across restarts
- No ads, no tracking, open source

<b>Please note</b>
Use responsibly: sending unsolicited bulk SMS may be illegal and may incur costs depending on your mobile plan. The app sends exactly one SMS per interval to the number you configured.
